FAQ

Good questions.

How much is your fee?+

A per-ticket service fee on top of the face value. You get the full breakdown in writing before you pay.

When do I pay?+

Full payment upfront. You receive an Order ID and receipt straight away.

What if you can’t secure my tickets?+

If the quota is hit or your order isn’t secured, you get a full refund within 3 working days.

Are the tickets physical or e-tickets?+

Most of the time they’re e-tickets, transferred through the organiser’s official platform — unless the listing specifically says physical ticket.

Can we sit together?+

Yes — side-by-side seats are available from 2 tickets. Final seats are assigned by the organiser; we’ll note your preferred zone.

What about name-bound or ID-checked tickets?+

Some events require the ticket name to match your ID. For those, Buy For You purchases in your name where the organiser allows it. We’ll tell you the rules for each event before you pay.
